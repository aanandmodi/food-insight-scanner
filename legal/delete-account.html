Food Insight

Delete your Food Insight account

Deleting your account removes your Food Insight profile, meal and scan history, shopping items, meal plans, account images and sign-in credentials. A minimal deletion guard may remain to prevent a previously issued token from recreating records. Provider operational logs may be retained under provider policies.

In the app

Open Food Insight → Settings → Delete Account. Confirm the action and, if requested, sign in again. Wait for the confirmation before removing the app.

Without the app

Send an account deletion request from the email address associated with your account to aanandmodi09@gmail.com with the subject “Food Insight account deletion”. If you used an anonymous account or no longer control your sign-in email, describe how we can identify your account without sending a password or medical details. We will verify ownership before deleting records and respond with the outcome. Do not email passwords or sensitive meal details.

Deleting the app alone does not request server account deletion. If in-app deletion fails, please use the email option above.
