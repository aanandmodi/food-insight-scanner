Food Insight

Privacy Policy

Last updated 30 September 2026

Food Insight Scanner ("Food Insight") helps you understand packaged food and keep a personal nutrition record. Contact aanandmodi09@gmail.com with privacy questions. The app is operated by the developer identified in its Google Play listing.

Information you provide

Account information includes your email address or Google identity, or an anonymous Firebase account. You may add name, age, height, weight, dietary preferences, allergies, health conditions, goals, meal entries, shopping items, and AI questions. Some of this information is sensitive health information. Profile fields are used for nutrition targets and personal Food Fit explanations; you can leave optional fields blank. Barcode scans and saved product history are linked to your account.

Health Connect and device activity

With your explicit permission, Food Insight reads steps and active calories from Health Connect to display daily activity and save daily totals to your account. The phone step counter is an alternative; calories and distance from step counts are estimates. These permissions are not needed for scanning. Manage or revoke Health Connect access in Android settings. We do not use Health Connect data for advertising or sell it.

Camera, product sources and AI

Camera access is used to detect barcodes on your device. Product barcodes are requested from our authenticated backend, which looks up community-contributed facts from Open Food Facts. Open Food Facts may receive the barcode and normal network metadata. Product records and images can be incomplete or outdated. AI requests are sent through our backend (hosted on Supabase Edge Functions) to Groq; the request may contain the question, selected product facts and the profile details needed for that task. Do not include information about other people in AI questions. AI replies may be inaccurate.

Where information is stored

Firebase Authentication manages your sign-in. Firebase Firestore and Supabase store account-linked records; Firebase Storage or Supabase Storage may hold user images. The app caches some records in on-device SQLite and preferences. Firebase Crashlytics receives minimized crash diagnostics when crash reporting is enabled in Settings. Diagnostic data can include app, device and stack information; our reporting code strips exception messages where practical. Service providers process data to operate the app under their own terms.

Optional usage analytics

Usage analytics is off by default. If you enable it in Settings, Firebase Analytics collects feature events (such as scan success or an AI limit being reached), basic app/device information and a pseudonymous app-instance identifier. We do not attach account IDs, barcodes, meals, allergies, health readings or chat content. Advertising identifiers and advertising personalization are disabled. Turning analytics off stops collection and resets the local analytics identifier; already collected provider data follows its retention settings.

Retention, control and deletion

Account data is kept while the account is active. Account-isolated local cache may remain after sign-out so it is available if the same account returns. Account deletion or app removal clears that local cache. In Settings you can delete your account; this removes account-linked records, user images and authentication credentials through our backend. Some minimal deletion guard metadata may remain to reject stale sign-in tokens. Provider operational logs and legally required records may follow their own retention periods. If you no longer have the app, use the account deletion page to request deletion. You can disable crash reporting in Settings.

Food and medical safety

Food Insight provides general nutrition information, not medical advice or a medical device. If you have a serious allergy, verify the physical package label before consuming a product. Missing data never proves a food is safe.
